The Croatian Wine Atlas is an independent, bilingual guide to Croatian wineries, grape varieties and wine regions. It is built for travellers planning a visit, for sommeliers and journalists researching Croatian wine, and for winemakers who want to be found.

## What makes it different

- **Sourced information.** Every winery profile shows where its information comes from and how it was verified.
- **The official map of Croatian wine.** Wineries are organised by the official regions, subregions and wine-growing districts defined in Croatian regulations.
- **Equal visibility.** Small family producers appear alongside large wineries. Editors' picks are never paid for, and any sponsored listing is clearly labelled.
- **Direct contact.** Visitors send tasting requests straight to wineries, without creating an account.

## Our data rules

We would rather show less than show something untrue. We do not invent vineyard statistics, prices, awards, opening hours or stories. Missing information is shown as missing.

## Coverage

The Atlas does not yet list every Croatian winery. Records are being added and verified region by region — see the coverage page for current numbers.

## For wineries

Listings are free. Owners can claim their profile to add wines, tasting experiences, photos and stories in Croatian and English.

## What we process and why

- **Tasting requests.** Name, email, optional phone and country, requested date, group size, language and your message. We use them to pass your request to the winery you chose and to send you a confirmation. Legal basis: steps taken at your request prior to a possible contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- **Accounts (optional).** Email, name, password hash, saved wineries, routes and visit history. Visit history is private to you. Legal basis: contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
- **Winery owners.** Contact details and evidence submitted with an ownership claim, used to verify the claim. Legal basis: legitimate interest in preventing listing takeovers (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Error reports and contact messages.** The message and, if you give it, your email.
- **Security.** We store a keyed hash of your IP address (never the raw address) to prevent spam and abuse.

## Who receives your data

Tasting requests are shared with the winery you contact. Email delivery may use a transactional email provider acting as our processor. We do not sell personal data.

## Retention

Tasting requests are kept for 24 months by default and then deleted or anonymised. Accounts are kept until you delete them.

## Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. Saved favourites and route plans may be stored in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies; if that changes, we will ask for your consent first.

## Marketing

Newsletter consent is optional and separate from your tasting request. You can withdraw it at any time.

## Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing, and you may lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).

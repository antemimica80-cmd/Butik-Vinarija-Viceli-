## The service

The Croatian Wine Atlas provides information about Croatian wineries, wines, grape varieties and wine regions, and lets visitors send tasting requests to wineries. It does not sell alcohol.

## Tasting requests

A tasting request is not a booking. Arrangements, prices and availability are agreed directly between you and the winery. Wine tastings are intended for adults of legal drinking age.

## Accuracy

We work to keep information accurate and show the source and verification status of key facts. Information may still be incomplete or out of date — please confirm details with the winery and report errors to us.

## Winery content

Wineries that claim their listing are responsible for the information, images and documents they submit, and confirm that they hold the rights to publish them. Editors may review, edit or decline submissions.

Do not misuse the service, submit false claims, scrape it at scale or attempt to access accounts or data that are not yours.
